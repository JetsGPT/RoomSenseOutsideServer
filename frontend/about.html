About RoomSense

Empowering smart spaces through intelligent environmental monitoring

Our Mission

RoomSense aims to bridge the gap between buildings and their occupants by providing precise, real-time environmental data. We believe that understanding your environment is the first step to improving it.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every building is smart, efficient, and comfortable. Through our IoT solutions, we're making this vision a reality—one room at a time.

What We Measure

Comprehensive environmental monitoring for complete awareness

Temperature

Precise temperature readings with ±0.5°C accuracy for optimal comfort monitoring.

Humidity

Track relative humidity levels to prevent mold growth and ensure comfort.

Air Quality

Monitor CO2, VOCs, and particulate matter for healthier indoor air.

Noise Levels

Detect ambient noise levels to identify disturbances and peak quiet hours.

Occupancy

Smart presence detection for energy optimization and space utilization insights.
